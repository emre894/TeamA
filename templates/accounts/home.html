SKY Health Check

Measure and monitor your team's health

What is Health Check?

Health Check is a technique developed by Sky and used to assess the health of teams. Engineering teams discuss and rate the health of critical areas in the team's operations (Code Base Health, Testing, Release Processes, Stakeholder Relations, etc.).

This application is designed to record, visualize, and track the progress of teams' health status over time.

How It Works

  1. Team members vote red, amber, or green for each health card.
  2. Progress trend (getting better, same, getting worse) is indicated for each card.
  3. Team leaders visualize team performance and track progress over time.

Red

Improvement needed

Amber

Needs attention

Green

Good condition
